SELF STUDY REFERENCE

Technical Self Study Curriculum

Prepared for Teo Espero

143 course entries across 17 study stages

This curriculum combines the supplied certificate programs into one self-study sequence. It begins with computing and programming, progresses through networks, security, GIS, and artificial intelligence, and then develops electronics, controls, design, manufacturing, and specialized subjects.

Read each table from top to bottom. Course descriptions use the course titles, with some punctuation simplified. Focus areas describe the recommended self-study emphasis. Books are independent recommendations, not the colleges’ assigned textbooks. Repeated titles can be reused across courses. Cross-listed courses share a row; electives and alternative courses are included.

Refresh algebra, trigonometry, unit conversions, and basic statistics as needed. Before advanced machine learning, add introductory linear algebra. Suggested references are OpenStax Algebra and Trigonometry, OpenStax Introductory Statistics, and Gilbert Strang’s Introduction to Linear Algebra.

Demonstrate familiar skills with exercises or a project before moving on. For new topics, work through problems and complete a small practical task. Use simulation and low-voltage equipment for independent practice; mains wiring, operating machinery, and patient-connected equipment require appropriate supervised training.

Running project

Use a simulated water utility as the shared project. Design its network, administer its servers, secure it, map its assets, analyze consumption, and build a low-voltage tank-level monitoring prototype. Add a CAD enclosure, solar power model, and operating dashboard as your skills develop.
