# Technical Self Study Curriculum

## Introduction

This self-study curriculum develops technical skills from computing and programming through networking, cybersecurity, GIS, artificial intelligence, electronics, controls, design, manufacturing, and specialized applications. It contains 143 course entries arranged across 17 study stages.

Start with the stage links below. Each stage is a major topic note with its courses listed in study order. Each course opens a separate note containing its focus, suggested textbook, study checklist, and space for notes and projects.

Move quickly through familiar subjects by completing exercises or demonstrating the skill. Spend more time on new topics and finish a small practical project before advancing. Refresh algebra, trigonometry, unit conversions, statistics, and linear algebra as needed.

Textbooks are independent self-study suggestions rather than the colleges’ assigned books. Related courses reuse references. This reading plan does not confer college credit or replace supervised practical training.

## Practical work

Use a shared project to connect your learning: a simulated utility network with servers, security controls, GIS assets, data analysis, and a low-voltage sensor prototype. Add design, fabrication, and a monitoring dashboard as your skills develop.
